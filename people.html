PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Jia Yang

Assistant Professor

Department of Natural Resource Ecology and Management
Oklahoma State University

jia.yang11@okstate.eduGoogle Scholar ↗OSU Experts ↗

Jia Yang studies wildfire dynamics and risk, water resources, and ecosystem responses to climate change and disturbance. His research integrates ecological modeling, Earth observation, geospatial analysis, and AI to understand interactions among fire, vegetation, water, and carbon and inform natural resource management.

He joined Oklahoma State University in January 2022. Previously, he served as an assistant professor at Mississippi State University and held research appointments at Auburn University.
