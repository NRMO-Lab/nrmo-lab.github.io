PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Jia Yang

Assistant Professor

Department of Natural Resource Ecology and Management
Oklahoma State University

jia.yang11@okstate.edu

Jia Yang studies fire ecology, wildfire risk and forecasting, and the impacts of climate change and disturbances on land ecosystems. His work combines ecological modeling, GIS, and remote sensing to investigate the connections among wildfire, vegetation, water, and carbon.

He joined Oklahoma State University in January 2022, following an assistant professor appointment at Mississippi State University and research appointments at Auburn University.
