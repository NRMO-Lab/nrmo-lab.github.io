Watersheds and Climate Change

How will changing climate and land cover reshape river flow and water availability?

Watershed modelingClimate scenariosStreamflow

2023 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Projecting river flow across the Arkansas River Basin

We projected twenty-first-century river flow across the Arkansas River Basin to investigate how warming and drying may affect water availability. The study identifies declining flow across the major rivers, with particularly strong reductions in the western and southwestern basin, connecting climate change with regional water-resource planning.

Publication details

Yang, J., Zou, C., Will, R., Wagner, K., Ouyang, Y., King, C., Winrich, A. and Tian, H., (2023). River flow decline across the entire Arkansas River Basin in the 21st century. Journal of Hydrology, 618, p.129253.

Read the paper ↗

2024 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Linking forest expansion with streamflow in Oklahoma

Using SWAT+ simulations in the Little River Watershed, we examined the combined effects of future climate and forest expansion on streamflow. The work connects woody vegetation management with water availability and inflows to Lake Thunderbird, highlighting the implications of changing land cover for water supply.

Publication details

Yang, J., Winrich, A., Zhang, T., Qiao, L., Mattingly, C., & Zou, C. (2024). Responses of streamflow to forest expansion in a typical subhumid watershed under future climate conditions. Journal of Environmental Management, 357, 120780.

Forests and Water Resources

What do forest restoration and land cover change mean for water storage and recharge?

AfforestationWater storageGroundwater recharge

2026 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Evaluating hydrological trade-offs of afforestation

Using SWAT+ simulations in the Lower Mississippi Alluvial Valley, we examined evapotranspiration, groundwater recharge, and water yield under future climate and afforestation scenarios. Afforestation can increase shallow groundwater recharge while reducing water yield, highlighting trade-offs for forest restoration and water management.

Publication details

† Wu, Q. Yang, J., Ouyang, Y., Siegert, C. Li, P., Song, J. (2026) Responses of Hydrological Processes to Future Climate Variability and Agricultural Land Reforestation in a Humid River Basin, Ecohydrology, 19, e70276

Read the paper ↗

2025 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Understanding how land cover influences water storage

We compared water storage capacity among forests and other land cover types, distinguishing observed storage from intrinsic capacity after accounting for environmental influences. The study shows why climate, terrain, soils, and evapotranspiration must be considered when evaluating the water-related benefits of forests.

Publication details

† Wu, Q., Yang, J., Song, J., Sun, H., Wang, H., & Xing, L. (2025). Do forests store more water? A comparative analysis of water storage between multiple land cover types. Journal of Hydrology, 133752.

Irrigation and Agricultural Water Use

How will agricultural water requirements change in a warming climate?

Crop water requirementsClimate projectionsIrrigation planning

2026 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Anticipating irrigation needs across U.S. croplands

We projected irrigation water requirements for major field crops across the contiguous United States under future climate scenarios. The study identifies increasing requirements during the twenty-first century, with the Southern Great Plains and Midwest emerging as important regions for future irrigation planning.

Publication details

† Yang, H., Yang, J., Ochsner, T. E., Zhang, Q., Jiao, X., Fang, S., ... & Zou, C. B. (2026). Increasing irrigation water requirements across croplands in the Contiguous United States throughout the 21st century. Agricultural Water Management, 323, 110044.

Read the paper ↗

2019 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Assessing future irrigation demand in the Mississippi Alluvial Valley

We investigated future irrigation water requirements across the Lower Mississippi Alluvial Valley. By examining agricultural water demand under changing climate conditions, this work connects crop production with long-term planning for water resources in a major agricultural region.

Publication details

Yang, J., Ren, W., Ouyang, Y., Feng, G., Tao, B., Granger, J.J. and Poudel, K.P., (2019). Projection of 21st century irrigation water requirement across the Lower Mississippi Alluvial Valley. Agricultural Water Management, 217, pp.60-72.

Soil Moisture and Evaporation

How can observations and models improve our understanding of land-surface water dynamics?

Earth observationSpatiotemporal datasetsEvaporation estimation

2025 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Mapping soil moisture at finer spatial and temporal scales

We developed a surface soil moisture dataset for the contiguous United States covering 2015–2023 at 1 km spatial resolution and three-hour intervals. The study combines spatial detail and frequent observations to support analysis of rapidly changing land-surface water conditions.

Publication details

† Yang, H., Yang, J., Ochsner, T. E., Krueger, E. S., Xu, M., & Zou, C. B. (2025). A 3 h, 1 km surface soil moisture dataset for the contiguous United States from 2015 to 2023. Earth System Science Data, 17(7), 3391-3409.

Read the paper ↗

2024 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Improving daily evaporation estimation

We improved a method that blends the evaporation–precipitation ratio with a complementary principle function to estimate evaporation. The work addresses calibration of the equilibrium evaporation coefficient and evaluates estimation performance, including under extreme climate conditions.

Publication details

† Wu, Q., Yang, J., Song, J., & Xing, L. (2024). Improvement in the blending the evaporation precipitation ratio with complementary principle function for daily evaporation estimation. Journal of Hydrology, 635, 131170.

Riverine Carbon and Nutrient Transport

How do climate and human activities influence what rivers carry downstream?

Ecosystem modelingCarbon exportNitrogen loading

2020 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Tracing nitrogen from the Mississippi Basin to the coast

This collaborative study uses the Dynamic Land Ecosystem Model to examine long-term nitrogen loading and delivery from the Mississippi River Basin to the Gulf of Mexico. It evaluates natural and human influences, including fertilizer, atmospheric deposition, land use, and climate, linking watershed processes with downstream water quality.

Publication details

Tian, H., Xu, R., Pan, S., Yao, Y., Bian, Z., Cai, W.J., Hopkinson, C.S., Justic, D., Lohrenz, S., Lu, C. Ren, W., Yang, J. (2020) Long‐Term Trajectory of Nitrogen Loading and Delivery From Mississippi River Basin to the Gulf of Mexico. Global Biogeochemical Cycles, 34(5), p.e2019GB006475.

Read the paper ↗

2016 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION

Connecting environmental change with riverine carbon export

This collaborative work examines the century-long trend and variability of dissolved organic carbon export from the Mississippi River Basin. It investigates natural and human influences on carbon transport, connecting terrestrial ecosystem processes with river networks and downstream carbon delivery.

Publication details

Ren, W., Tian, H., Cai, W., Lohrenz, S., Hopkinson, C., Huang, W., Yang, J., Tao, B., Pan, S., He, R. (2016) Century-long increasing trend and variability of dissolved organic carbon export from the Mississippi River basin driven by natural and anthropogenic forcing, Global Biogeochemical Cycles, 30(9), 1288-1299
